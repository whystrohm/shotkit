# Shotkit 3.4.0: Your own footage, one install, and a person's approval

- **Start from your own photos and clips.** `sk media scan <folder> --out output/`. Claude looks at each file, places it on a shot and marks what is missing. A generator fills only the gaps.
- **One install.** `/plugin install shotkit --marketplace whystrohm/shotkit`, then type a brief.
- **Point it at your site** to get the brand lock. No table to fill in.
- **A person's approval.** `sk approve output/ --by "Name"` records who approved the plan or the frames, when, and what, by hash. The board says when an approval no longer holds.
- **Sound in the plan.** Music mood and sound effects as intent for the edit. `sk generate --sound` asks a video model for sound without touching the pinned plan.
- **Type like the brand's**, safe zones on vertical video, and text you can drag on a live board.
- **No sales message on what you make.** The board footer and the client page carry none.

Fixed: the open-shot panel keeps its Close button on a phone; no type under 11px; the README says what leaves your computer; the Harbor Roast example is checked on every change.
