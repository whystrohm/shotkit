# Brand Lock: Oriel (fictional brand)

Oriel is a fictional fragrance house made for this demo. It is not a real company, and
no client assets were used. Every value below was written for the demo.

## Identity

**Brand:** Oriel
**One-line description:** A fictional small fragrance house that makes a few eaux de parfum, sold in plain glass bottles with a brass cap, and shows them in quiet rooms at the end of the day rather than on models.
**Archetype:** Creator
**Voice posture:** Quiet and exact. Short declaratives that name the scent, the notes and the hour, and never tell the reader how to feel.

## Palette

Every hex value here is allowed. Anything outside this list is not.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#14110F` | Umber black, primary canvas |
| Ink | `#EFE6D8` | Bone, primary text on dark |
| Accent | `#B8862D` | Brass, the one accent, one element per frame |
| Muted | `#8E847A` | Stone grey, secondary text and captions |
| Rule | `#2B2520` | Ash, borders and dividers |

## Typography

**Display font:** `Cormorant Garamond SemiBold 600`
**Body font:** `Inter Regular 400`

## Mood adjectives

- end-of-day quiet, not sleepy
- warm, not golden-hour glossy
- a lived-in room, not a set
- close and tactile, not aspirational

## Never list

- never a face; people appear as a wrist, a hand or the back of a neck
- never a model posing with the bottle toward the camera
- never flowers, petals or fruit arranged around the bottle
- never bright white product-on-white shots
- never more than one brass accent element in a frame
- never a legible label, logo or text inside a generated image; all copy is composited
- never exclamation points
- never the words "luxury", "iconic", "exclusive" or "signature"

## Aspect ratios

Default: 9:16 for Instagram, TikTok and Shorts. 4:5 for feed posts. 16:9 for the site.

## Color grade direction

> Muted and warm, blacks lifted just off crush, bone highlights, one brass accent, soft halation on the light source, fine grain. One low raking source and deep cool shadow, in the manner of a Hammershøi interior at dusk.

## Motion language

> Slow and held. Static frames or one slow dolly per shot. Cuts land on an action: a cap lifted, a mist released, a curtain lifting. Type fades in and sits still. No whip pans, no speed ramps, no transitions beyond a cut.

## Voice rules

- no exclamation points, anywhere
- never "luxury", "iconic", "exclusive", "signature" or "curated"
- sentences under eight words on screen
- name the scent, the notes or the hour rather than a feeling
- no questions in on-screen copy
- no price, size or claim that the brief did not give
