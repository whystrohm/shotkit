## Brief

The brief, as given: "15-second 9:16 Instagram ad for Harbor Roast's new fall roast, Fog Line. No voiceover. Use brand/harbor-roast.md as the brand lock."

Harbor Roast is a fictional brand made for this example.

A vertical social ad announcing a limited seasonal roast for a fictional small-batch coffee roaster. The audience is people who already buy whole-bean coffee and scroll with the sound off, so every line is on screen and none is spoken. The goal is to name the roast, say what it tastes like, and send people to the shop before the batch runs out.

Visual world: a small roastery by the water at first light. Dark walnut, black steel, fog in the windows, one amber light. People appear only as hands or from behind. No legible text, labels or logos in any frame; the roast name, the taste notes and the call to order are composited from `text-overlays.json` in Fraunces and Work Sans.

The brand lock was built by brand-lock-extractor from a written description (`brand/harbor-roast-description.md`); its Extraction notes list the values still waiting on confirmation.

## Beat framework: Hero Trilogy

A product launch: the roast is the hero. At 15s World is one shot and Transformation carries the CTA.

- 0.0 to 3.5s. World (fog season, one cup, hook in the first 2s)
- 3.5 to 9.5s. Hero (the roast drops out of the drum, then the beans up close with the taste notes)
- 9.5 to 15.0s. Transformation (the first cup of the new roast, then the bag with the CTA)

World runs 3.5s rather than the framework's 4s and Hero ends at 9.5s rather than 10s, so the CTA shot gets the full 3s the timing rules ask for at 15s.
