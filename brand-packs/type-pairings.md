# Type pairings

For a brand that has no typeface yet, or only says "something clean". Each pairing is
free on Google Fonts, so the board and the preview load it with no setup. Pick by what
the brand is, not by taste, and write the two fonts into the brand lock's Typography
section. A brand that already has type keeps its own.

| Feel | Display | Body | Suits | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Trade, direct | `Manrope ExtraBold 800` | `Manrope Regular 400` | Electricians, plumbers, HVAC, clinics | Sentence case, tight |
| Trade, loud | `Barlow Condensed Bold 700` | `Barlow Regular 400` | Roofing, paving, fleets, gyms | Capitals work, four words or fewer |
| Craft, warm | `Fraunces SemiBold 600` | `Work Sans Regular 400` | Coffee, bakeries, makers, hospitality | Sentence case, centred |
| Editorial | `Newsreader SemiBold 600` | `Inter Regular 400` | Firms, schools, publishers, health | Sentence case, left |
| Studio, sharp | `Archivo Bold 700 wdth 80` | `IBM Plex Mono Regular 400` | Agencies, software, media | Tight, left, mono for labels |
| Modern, friendly | `Bricolage Grotesque Bold 700` | `Figtree Regular 400` | Consumer apps, food brands, retail | Sentence case |
| Premium, quiet | `Cormorant Garamond SemiBold 600` | `Jost Regular 400` | Interiors, jewellery, wine, real estate | Large, with room around it |
| Technical | `Sora SemiBold 600` | `IBM Plex Sans Regular 400` | Engineering, energy, logistics | Sentence case, numbers read well |
| Outdoor, sturdy | `Red Hat Display ExtraBold 800` | `Red Hat Text Regular 400` | Landscaping, pools, builders | Sentence case |
| Bold retail | `Anton Regular 400` | `Inter Regular 400` | Sales, events, drops | Capitals, three words or fewer |

## Rules

- Two fonts per brand: one for lines, one for labels and small text.
- The display font's weight is part of the brand. Do not mix bold and light headlines
  in one video.
- A serif display needs a larger size than a sans to read at the same distance. Use
  `display` or `headline`, not `body`.
- A condensed face can carry more words per line. It still obeys the word limits.
- Check the pairing over a real frame on the board before the prompts are written.
