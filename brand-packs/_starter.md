# Brand Lock: Starter

> A neutral, filled-in brand lock. Shotkit uses it when a brief names no brand, so a
> first run produces a storyboard, a board and valid files without stopping to ask for
> colours. It is not your brand. Replace it before real work: ask Claude to
> "write a brand lock from <your site>", or copy `_template.md` and fill it in.

## Identity

**Brand:** Starter (no brand set)
**One-line description:** A placeholder identity for a first run. It stands in until a real brand lock is supplied.
**Archetype:** Everyman
**Voice posture:** Plain and direct. Short sentences that say what the thing is.

## Palette

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Background | `#0E1013` | Near-black, primary canvas |
| Ink | `#F3F2EE` | Warm white, primary text |
| Accent | `#FF5A2B` | Signal orange, one element per frame |
| Muted | `#9096A0` | Grey, secondary text and captions |
| Rule | `#22262D` | Dark grey, borders and dividers |

## Typography

**Display font:** `Inter Bold 700`
**Body font:** `Inter Regular 400`

## Mood adjectives

- clear
- calm
- direct
- unbranded

## Never list

- never show a logo, wordmark or product name that the brief did not supply
- never bake text into a generated frame
- never use more than one accent element in a frame
- never use stock-photo staging or uncanny faces

## Aspect ratios

- 16:9, default
- 9:16, short-form social
- 1:1, feed posts

## Color grade direction

Neutral and natural. Whites clean, blacks deep but not crushed, skin tones true. No heavy
stylisation, so the frames survive a later move to the real brand's grade.

## Motion language

Restrained. Static frames and slow pushes. Hard cuts on the beat. No whip pans and no
transitions beyond a cut.

## Voice rules

- no em dashes
- no exclamation points
- no hype words
- prefer specific facts from the brief over general claims
- never state a fact, a number or a customer the brief did not give
